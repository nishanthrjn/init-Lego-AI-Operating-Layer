## 1. Context and scope

The fourteen governing documents were reviewed in authority order. ADR-001 through ADR-005 are accepted. Python/CPython, package boundaries, immutable configuration, owner-local consistency and explicit security context remain closed. The canonical dependencies are ADR-004 and ADR-005; ADR-006 and ADR-007 are consumers of this decision, not new prerequisites for writing it.

## 2. Decision question and distinctions

How does the runtime create, acknowledge, retain, protect, discover and repair mandatory evidence when source facts and evidence custody commit independently?

| Concept | Meaning preserved |
|---|---|
| Source fact | Authoritative statement at its domain owner about a decision, transition or observed effect |
| Evidence requirement | Attributable obligation specifying what evidence and guarantee are needed, when and for which subject; not the resulting record |
| Evidence record | Scoped retained assertion/observation with exact source meaning, provenance and custody metadata |
| Custody acknowledgment | Evidence owner's bounded commitment about a particular record and guarantee; not the source decision or proof the assertion is true |
| Log/metric/trace | Observation optimized for operations; evidence only when explicitly admitted into required custody with applicable guarantees |
| Event | Fact/message with its own delivery semantics; emission/receipt alone is not evidence custody |
| Audit/general provenance | Attributable decision/action and lineage evidence; source business state and Artifact relationships remain with their respective owners |
| Evaluation/conformance result | Judgment under its own subject/method/criteria; may be held as evidence but is not automatically mandatory operational evidence, authorization or certification |
| Permission/Approval | Independently governed action authority/use decision; retained evidence does not issue or restore either |
| Repair obligation | Durable outstanding work under CONTRACTS §28; not a new Execution state |

Evidence is neither business state nor a log line, metric, trace, Event, EvaluationResult, conformance finding, Permission, Approval, authorization or private chain-of-thought. Records can reference these things without acquiring their semantics. Custody of an incorrect assertion preserves an attributable assertion; it does not turn it into a true fact.

An Observability record is not an Evidence record: emitting a log does not establish custody, and a trace's existence does not satisfy mandatory evidence. A log/trace becomes evidence only through explicit admission into Evidence custody under the required guarantee. ADR-013 cannot override this boundary. Likewise, Event emission is not custody acknowledgment; Events may transport or reference evidence without requiring a broker.

An Evaluation result is not authority, Permission or Conformance. Its admission into custody does not make its judgment infallible. Conformance may consume retained evidence, but custody alone does not empower Evidence to decide conformant, non-conformant or certified. Such responsibility would require an explicit canonical delegation; no certification authority is introduced here.

## 3. Alternatives considered

### A — Source-local evidence with governed export

Each source owner durably commits necessary evidence with its own state and later exports it. This gives simple local atomicity and crash-safe submission material. It is sufficient only when the governing requirement expressly accepts that source-local custody and its actual guarantees. A later export cannot meet a requirement for independent Evidence-owner custody before effect.

As the universal first mechanism, reject A: it duplicates custody/access/retention behavior across business owners and risks mistaking a source commit for required audit custody. Its durable source-fact preparation is retained as part of C, with no claim that the preparation itself satisfies a different custodian requirement.

### B — Independently durable Evidence owner with an acknowledgment barrier

Sources submit records to a distinct Evidence owner and wait for the required acknowledgment. This gives a clear custody boundary and is sufficient for pre-effect evidence when its guarantee is valid at handoff. It supports separate physical storage but does not require it.

Alone it leaves a crash gap for post-effect submission if the sender keeps pending work only in memory: the effect can occur, then the process dies before submitting or recording responsibility. Adding durable source obligations addresses that gap. The Evidence owner cannot reconstruct every missing source observation from a custody request it never received.

### C — Durable source obligations plus explicit Evidence-owner custody

Sources durably retain their facts, submission material/references and obligations under ADR-004. A distinct Evidence owner validates and commits custody under its own owner-local boundary. Required pre-effect custody is an actual barrier; post-effect failure leaves a recoverable obligation, never a repeated business effect.

**Select C.** It combines B's explicit custody acknowledgment with the source durability already selected in ADR-004. It introduces no universal event log, distributed transaction, graph database, broker or generic Saga framework. It is the simplest option that covers both required timing cases without relying on process survival.

| Criterion | A — Source-local/export | B — Custodian/barrier alone | C — Selected hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory prior custody | Only if source custodian is explicitly sufficient | Explicit barrier | Explicit barrier, no substitution by local obligation |
| Source/evidence ownership | Easy to conflate | Clear separation | Clear separation with linked facts |
| Post-effect crash | Source commit can preserve work | Sender gap unless independently retained | Durable source intent/facts/obligations |
| Lost custody response | Requires result tracking at each custodian | Evidence owner result inquiry | Same inquiry plus recoverable sender obligation |
| Confidentiality/retention | Repeated across many source implementations | Central logical custody contracts | Shared custody rules, source copies independently governed |
| Integrity and attribution | Depends on source qualification | Custodian must validate source authority | Source and custody attestations remain distinguishable |
| Solo operation | Few handoffs but dispersed duties | Simple custody boundary, incomplete alone | More records, one local backing realization sufficient |
| Replaceability | Hard if business records become audit API | Clear Evidence boundary | Same boundary plus explicit sender recovery |
| Migration cost | Many custodians and export coverage | Custody records/results | Custody plus outstanding source obligations; honest completeness |

These are architectural judgments, not measured performance or assurance scores. An asynchronous observability pipeline is not an additional viable mandatory-custody alternative unless it actually satisfies the same admission, commit and acknowledgment contract.

## 4. Decision

Use **durable owner-local source facts and evidence obligations, a separately authoritative Evidence custodian, explicit durable custody acknowledgments, and governed reconciliation**.

1. Applicable authority specifies evidence class, exact subject/scope, responsible source/custodian, timing, required acknowledgment, failure/recovery/retention/access/integrity guarantees and validity/deadline.
2. The source or responsible coordinator records submission responsibility and necessary exact input references durably before an effectful handoff can leave an untracked gap.
3. Evidence validates the submitter, subject/source, scope, interpretation, evidence meaning and requested guarantee. It atomically commits the immutable record, custody metadata, retained submission outcome and any owner-local obligations before acknowledging durable custody.
4. A required pre-effect acknowledgment must be established and applicable before the effect is released. Unknown, queued or rejected custody does not satisfy it.
5. Post-effect custody failure preserves actual effect status and a durable repair obligation. Repair submits/reconciles evidence, not the original producing effect.
6. Recovery inspects both owners' facts and obligations, resolves lost acknowledgments and preserves explicit missing/unverifiable evidence. No global transaction or automatic history reconstruction is assumed.

Initially use ADR-004's one local persistence realization where suitable, with separate logical Evidence ownership and owner-local commits. No store product, schema, signing/hash algorithm, logging framework, wire format or storage API is selected. Independently durable means its own authoritative durability contract, not physical fault independence from a shared disk.

## 5. Ownership, requirements and timing

Evidence and Provenance owns evidence identity, custody, declared durability, integrity/protection metadata, access enforcement, applicable retention/disposition, custody acknowledgments and its own repair status. It does not own Execution, Grant/Policy, Plugin lifecycle, resource balances, configuration or Artifact business meaning. It applies policies from their entitled owners; it does not decide disputed retention authority.

For example, Plugin Lifecycle commits activation A active at L8. Evidence E17 records an attributable observation of A/L8. E17 cannot activate, stop or authorize use of A. A later Plugin transition does not rewrite E17, and E17's existence is not current eligibility. Artifact relationship mutations remain Artifact-owned; general provenance may reference those exact edges.

Mandatory evidence derives from accepted contracts and applicable entitled Policy/control requirements: security decisions, Approval/resource duties, Plugin admission, protected effects, retention obligations or scoped release/conformance claims. No new universal category or official profile is imposed. A conformance claim can require retained proof without retroactively making every operational log mandatory. Mandatory status and any permitted omission must be attributable; a telemetry setting cannot disable it.

The conceptual requirement is expressed through existing operation/Policy/obligation contracts, not a new canonical entity. Unknown applicability is not “not required.” Missing material guarantee parameters block the affected requirement until its authority supplies them; no hidden default TTL or custody strength is invented.

| Timing | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Precondition evidence | Verify exact required evidence before the protected admission/read/mutation to which the rule applies |
| Pre-effect evidence | Obtain required custody acknowledgment, then establish actual applicability with all current handoff conditions |
| Post-effect evidence | Capture only known result/effect observations and submit; retain outstanding duty on failure |
| Continuous/stream evidence | Each supported bounded segment has attributable coverage and custody; a receipt cannot attest future/unreceived segments |
| Recovery/repair evidence | Append observations/corrections linked to the original record/obligation; no fictitious original capture time or replayed business effect |

The first implementation supports bounded records/segments. If a requirement demands continuous lossless capture that the realization cannot prove, dependent use is unsupported; treating a partial stream as complete is forbidden. Pauses/gaps are explicit. If continuous evidence is a prerequisite for later protected actions, an unfulfilled segment/control blocks those later actions under its rule, without erasing earlier effects.

Every completeness claim identifies the evidence requirements, operations, exact relevant versions, interval/tenant/subject scope, retained coverage and failure/retention assumptions it covers. There is no unqualified claim of a complete audit trail. Loss of explicitly optional telemetry does not by itself make mandatory evidence incomplete; a missing mandatory record or required supporting fact does. A scoped gap cannot be hidden by completeness in a different interval or evidence class.

## 6. Exact record meaning, identity and integrity

A record carries the applicable evidence identity, tenant/publication scope, source owner and exact record/revision, source operation/causal relationships, class/purpose, producer and custodian, event/recording times with uncertainty, interpretation versions, integrity status, access/retention basis, completeness, freshness, limitations and custody outcome. Material configuration, Plugin release/activation, Provider/Capability, Policy/Grant, Approval/claim and Artifact versions are pinned as relevant. Not every record needs every possible field.

Use an **Evidence-owner-issued scoped identity**. A submission can reserve/obtain that identity under the custodian's contract; its operation/result mapping must remain discoverable if the reply is lost. Do not infer identity from a filename, log offset, timestamp or content hash. No allocation algorithm is chosen. Evidence identity, custody submission operation identity and original source operation identity remain distinct and linked.

Source references identify exact historical meaning, never latest. Time aids chronology but does not create a global order: owner revisions, causal links, effective-order evidence and acknowledgments establish the relevant relation. Graph traversal uses ordinary scoped references; no universal graph datastore is needed.

An evidence record may contain a bounded retained assertion and safe source references. If its guarantee requires continued access to supporting content/interpretation, custody must include that content or a verified preservation arrangement meeting the requirement. A dangling external reference cannot support a claim of retained proof. Conversely, custody of a bounded attestation alone need not promise retention of every upstream payload when the accepted requirement only asks for that attestation; report precisely what was inspected and retained.

Evidence admission verifies current submitter authority, source entitlement/association, tenant, subject/revision, type and interpretation, completeness required by the obligation and actual available guarantee. Independently check source-owner facts through authorized exact references where required. A caller label “Plugin active” is not a source-owner attestation. If source verification is unavailable, record it as an unverified assertion only where an authorized record class allows that; it cannot satisfy a requirement for verified fact.

Required integrity properties are binding to source/subject/version, protected attributable writes, immutable recorded meaning, correction linkage, detection of unauthorized changes appropriate to the admitted threat model and explicit verifier behavior. The first scope assumes sufficiently trusted host/realization code as ADR-005 does. It must qualify corruption/integrity checks for the actual storage stack, but makes no Byzantine administrator or malicious-host tamper-proof claim. If a rule demands independent tamper evidence beyond that scope, refuse it until a suitable realization is qualified.

Optional digests/signatures may support a later implementation; a hash alone proves neither issuer authority nor the truth of content. No cryptographic algorithm or append-log design is selected. Existing records are not silently edited to fix inconvenient facts. Corrections append linked new records with their own producer, times and custody; original/counterevidence remains visible subject to access/retention policy. Correction does not retroactively claim the original assertion was correct.

## 7. Custody transaction and acknowledgment meaning

Use `record_audit_evidence` and `record_provenance` under CONTRACTS §25 as applicable. Evidence admission, expected-absence/revision or equivalent identity guards, immutable content/metadata, submission equivalence/outcome and required Evidence-owner obligations form one ADR-004 owner-local atomic set. Mutable disposition/status changes are revision guarded; published evidence content is not overwritten.

A successful durable custody acknowledgment names the exact record/revision and subject, custodian/realization, scope, accepted evidence class/interpretation, committed guarantee and retention/access/integrity/coverage limitations, plus submission identity and attributable commit evidence. It means the record crossed the declared durable boundary and is referenceable under that failure model and policy. It does not mean forever retained, always readable by this caller, universally tamper-proof, geographically replicated, legally compliant everywhere, a successful business effect or a satisfied conformance claim.

| Observed stage | Truthful interpretation |
|---|---|
| Evidence not required | Established applicable rules require none for this purpose; not inferred from absent settings |
| Required but not attempted | Obligation remains unsatisfied; pre-effect dependent action blocked |
| Custody rejected | Requested custody not accepted; preserve precise safe cause and outstanding duty |
| Dependency unavailable | Cannot establish needed custody; not proof no prior commit exists |
| Received/queued | Receipt only; not committed-recoverable custody |
| Custody result unknown | Commit may have happened; inspect same operation/record, no assumed failure or success |
| Durable custody acknowledged | Exact bounded guarantee established; actual handoff still checks applicability/current controls |
| Previously acknowledged, now unreadable | Historical acknowledgment remains a fact; retrieval/current requirement may be unavailable and needs disposition |
| Repair outstanding | Separate known duty remains; does not alter the canonical effect status |

These are descriptions using existing results/obligations, not new Evidence or Execution lifecycle states. Custody status, business result and effect status are independent. Canonical effects remain **none, partial, completed, unknown**. A completed external effect plus failed custody can yield incomplete parent postconditions; it never becomes “no effect” or a new effect enum such as completed-but-not-audited.

## 8. Pre-effect barrier and post-effect handoff

### 8.1 Pre-effect flow

1. Establish exact action, applicable evidence requirement, authorized context and current source facts. Persist required submission responsibility/material under its source/coordinator owner.
2. Submit to the Evidence owner and obtain the **specified** custody acknowledgment. If the result is unknown, query/reconcile before release.
3. Check record identity/subject/revisions, custodian entitlement, class, coverage, required durability/retention/integrity, validity and deadline against the requirement. Reject wrong-subject, obsolete or insufficient receipts.
4. Establish current joint applicability with authority, Approval, resources, effect identity and other mandatory controls at actual handoff. ADR-007 chooses that ordering mechanism. A receipt does not freeze grants, deadlines or evidence validity indefinitely.
5. Only then release the effect. Preparation delayed beyond coverage requires revalidation/renewed evidence as applicable, not unexamined reuse of the old receipt.

This is an explicit logical barrier, not a global transaction. A prerequisite record may remain in custody even when later dispatch is cancelled or denied; it attests preparation, not execution. Evidence of a future outcome cannot be manufactured to pass the barrier. Known later loss/revocation/expiry of necessary evidence blocks new dependent use unless the governing requirement is still actually met under its accepted guarantee; no indefinite evidence lease is invented here.

### 8.2 Post-effect flow and capture gap

Before effectful handoff, the invoking owner must durably record enough action/effect identity, safe input/source references and required post-effect evidence obligation to discover unfinished work after a crash. For a purely local source mutation, its new fact and submission obligation commit atomically with source state. This follows ADR-004, not a new global protocol.

After the effect, capture observed status/result in the source owner and submit the exact corresponding evidence. If the process dies between external effect and recording its response, the pre-existing intent/obligation preserves the need to investigate; it does not preserve response bytes never durably captured. Report unknown and query the authoritative provider/source under the later ADR-008 contract. Unrecoverable details are a truthful gap, not invented evidence. A duty requiring guaranteed capture of such details cannot be advertised until the provider/capture boundary demonstrably supports it.

If source fact committed but custody fails, source fact remains true, and its durable submission obligation remains discoverable. If custody commits but the source fails to record its receipt, reconcile by the same submission identity. Do not repeat the Tool call, reinitialize a Plugin, consume another Approval or charge resources merely to reconstruct an audit trail.

## 9. Responsibility, duplicates and repair

| Duty | Accountable owner |
|---|---|
| Produce/retain source fact and initiate required submission | Source owner, or explicitly designated invoking coordinator for the effect it owns |
| Retain unsatisfied submission/custody obligation | That source/responsible coordinator; designation is durable before the handoff |
| Validate, retain, acknowledge and expose evidence | Evidence owner |
| Repair Evidence storage/disposition/index faults | Evidence owner, with its own authorized obligations |
| Resolve Policy/retention conflict | Entitled governing authority; Evidence applies the resulting decision |
| Decide source effect reconciliation/compensation | Original invoking/source owners under their contracts, not Evidence |

Each obligation has CONTRACTS §28 identity, owner, responsible Principal/requester, tenant, parent operation/effect, required action/target revision, due/retention bound, guarantee, canonical obligation status, last evidence/error, next safe action and reconciliation authority. No “someone will export it later” ownership. Enumeration through authorized owner queries, not just an in-memory retry queue, makes pending work recoverable. A scheduler may be replaceable; it does not own the facts.

Source obligation closure requires the matching accepted custody guarantee or explicit entitled disposition of the remaining duty. An error log or successful submit call lacking the required acknowledgment cannot close it. Evidence cannot unilaterally waive the source requirement. Failed/requires-intervention obligations remain visible under policy with due/review rules, rather than silently disappearing or retrying forever.

Submission retransmission retains original contract/version, operation identity, tenant/subject/requester, exact record meaning, source revisions and preconditions under CTR-014–016. Equivalent committed retransmission returns the authorized prior outcome within declared coverage, even if owner state has advanced. Different content under the same evidence identity or different meaning under the same operation identity yields Conflict. Concurrent duplicates must be guarded in the custody transaction, not merely by a prior lookup.

Two separately attributable observations of the same source fact/purpose may legitimately be distinct evidence records. Preserve their producer/method/time/limitations; do not collapse them because content hashes match. A repeated transport of the same submission is different from a new observation or linked correction. For an already-known evidence identity, exact equivalent content can return the existing custody reference under authorized semantics; no duplicate business fact is created.

ADR-008 still chooses broader equivalence/effect identity and deduplication machinery. This ADR requires stable submission/result behavior under the accepted contracts, not a new global horizon or cross-provider exactly-once guarantee. Before implementation advertises retry coverage, its interval/failure domain must be explicit. Expired result coverage or missing retained records is not proof that no prior custody/effect occurred; reconcile or obtain authorized disposition, never blindly generate new semantic evidence to conceal uncertainty.

### 9.1 Repair authority and irrecoverable gaps

A repair worker uses a bounded service Principal, explicit TenantContext, current ADR-005 authorization and the exact outstanding obligation/revision. Its permitted source read, evidence submission/status inquiry and obligation update are independently scoped; no omnipotent internal repair identity exists. Authority to repair custody does not authorize changing source business state, repeating the producing effect or rewriting the original evidence. Concurrent repair/disposition uses owner-local expected revisions and retained outcomes, with bounded retries/resources/deadlines and escalation to the entitled responsible authority.

Repair uses the retained exact source fact and interpretation. It records original event/source time if known, its uncertainty, and separately the repair/submission/custody times, repair reason and missing inputs. If the necessary original fact is no longer recoverable, do not substitute latest state, fabricate details or replay the producing effect. Retain an explicit unresolved/irrecoverable limitation and seek governed disposition; use existing failed/requires-intervention obligation semantics rather than inventing an irrecoverable lifecycle state. Disposition can record that recovery is impossible, but cannot turn an unmet proof requirement into satisfied custody. Claims requiring that missing proof remain unsupported.

## 10. Failure model, restart and storage requirements

Custody uses ADR-004's durable owner-local boundary: committed records/results/required obligations survive process/runtime crash, normal storage-process restart and local machine termination/restart with intact qualified storage honoring its durable-write contract. Abrupt termination/flush behavior requires actual qualification. No device-loss, offsite disaster or malicious full-store rollback survival is promised.

| Failure point | Required recovery/failure truth |
|---|---|
| Before Evidence validation | No new custody established; required pre-effect gate stays closed |
| After validation, before durable commit | Validation is not custody; previous committed records remain |
| During custody commit | Qualified recovery yields the complete old or new owner-local set, not a torn accepted record |
| Commit succeeds, response lost | Record/outcome survive; caller initially has unknown custody and queries same operation |
| After acknowledged commit, process/store restart | Recover exact record/outcome and required interpretation within declared retention scope |
| Source restart before recording receipt | Recover source obligation and inspect Evidence status; no repeat producing action |
| Custody dependency unavailable | Pre-effect required use blocked; post-effect gap/obligation retained |
| Corrupt/unverifiable record | Do not return it as trustworthy proof; fail affected custody/use scope, preserve safe references and repair obligation |
| Storage rollback/backup restore | Gate affected use until identity, coverage, loss and current requirements are reconciled; an old valid row does not prove latest custody/history |
| Device/storage loss | Acknowledgment history may survive elsewhere but content may not; explicit loss/unavailability, no fabricated reconstruction |

Restart verifies Evidence identity/scope, committed completeness, exact interpretation and required integrity, then restores outcomes/obligations before dependent work. Source owners separately recover their facts. Reconcile cross-owner relationships; neither owner rewrites the other to make histories agree. Rebuildable indexes/projections remain non-authoritative. A missing store is not permission to initialize an empty custodian under the old identity.

Minimum storage behaviors: atomic conditional owner mutation, immutable record insertion and linked correction, scoped uniqueness/equivalence guards, durable record plus result/obligation commit, exact revision retrieval, coherent authoritative status, tenant/access enforcement, discoverable pending work, explicit versioned interpretation, governed retention/disposition and integrity verification appropriate to threat scope. Named database isolation levels, SQL, schemas, serialization formats and API classes remain unselected.

Shared physical storage reduces local setup and migration surfaces but introduces correlated failure/privilege domains. Separate physical stores could improve independent custody/fault isolation where required, at higher deployment and reconciliation cost. A requirement for independent physical custody is not satisfied merely by different logical namespaces in one store; unsupported stronger requirements block use.

A later co-located source/evidence transaction is permitted only as ADR-004's separately reviewed optimization, preserving each owner's validation and outward semantics. It is not required, does not authorize peer state writes and cannot become a canonical all-or-nothing source/custody guarantee. All separate-store partial cases remain supported. No distributed 2PC is introduced.

## 11. Confidentiality, retention and historical availability

Evidence reads, status, searches, provenance traversal, diagnostics and duplicate-result disclosure require current ADR-005 authority and exact tenant scope. Single-tenant operation still binds a tenant; possession of an evidence reference is not Permission. Public errors, restricted diagnostics, tenant-private content and authorized security-review views remain distinct. Index/count/existence queries must not leak foreign subjects, grants, tenants or topology.

Evidence search, counts, ID lookup, submission-status reconciliation, provenance traversal and repair views are all protected operations. Indexes and error differences must not disclose unauthorized tenant existence, Principal identity, resource existence, Grant presence, Plugin identity, SecretRef existence or evidence counts. Wrong/missing TenantContext is not corrected by inferring the record's tenant. An authorized internal reviewer receives additional detail only under that reviewer's own current scope; knowing an evidence or submission ID grants no access to a retained result.

Use minimal safe references and bounded claims. Ordinary custody/logging must not collect raw credentials, bearer tokens, private keys, full authentication assertions, Secret bytes or private model chain-of-thought. SecretRef/purpose/consumer/actual safe revision-use evidence is not Secret material; ADR-010 owns material handling. Permitted public rationale, structured decision, input/output references and evaluator result can explain behavior without hidden reasoning. Redacted evidence can satisfy a requirement only if its authority accepts the remaining proof scope; record the redaction and limits honestly.

Retention is explicit and attributable to governing authority, not a universal duration. Evidence acknowledges only a retention/recovery guarantee it can satisfy. Source copies and supporting content have their own governed retention/access arrangements; deleting a source does not silently edit an evidence copy, but lawful deletion obligations may independently require disposition of that copy. Conflicting deletion/hold/retention obligations follow SEC-024 and Q-05: block the conflicting action, preserve only permitted state and create accountable timely resolution/review duties. Default denial does not authorize indefinite retention.

Disposition uses authorized revisioned operations and safe minimal tombstones where permitted. Preserve the distinction between never-existing, known identity, retrievable record, retained content and supported interpretation. Where a tombstone is itself prohibited, identity non-reuse must still prevent an old reference resolving to different content. Do not retain sensitive deleted payload merely to make tombstones convenient. Later loss/expiry can weaken current proof availability without disproving a historical event or its original acknowledgment.

Changing a Policy/Grant or evidence requirement does not rewrite the old decision record. Historical authorization remains evidence of then, not authority for now. A historical record can be readable but unusable for current enforcement because its subject, guarantee, freshness or interpretation no longer meets the present requirement.

## 12. Finite evidence roots and bootstrap

Evidence obligations must terminate at a finite independently established accountable root under SEC-054–056 and CTR-061. The environment's entitled authority designates the initial Evidence duty with responsible Principal, explicit tenant, realization/version/configuration, allowed classes/actions/subjects, custody/access/retention/integrity guarantees, bounds, threat assumptions, dependencies, failure/recovery and replacement authority. The candidate does not become trustworthy because it writes “I am trusted.”

A root custody acknowledgment attests its own record under that already established duty. It need not demand an identical new audit approval/record recursively to attest itself. This is the explicit bounded root rule, not an evidence exemption the caller can choose. Requests outside the root's designation still require normal authority and controls; no unlimited SYSTEM Principal appears.

Initial trust-root designation, verifier trust, Evidence realization qualification and initial tenant/Principal establishment can have independently supplied protected source evidence. Record who designated the root and the independent basis/limitations; subsequently recording that designation supplies custody and lineage, not retroactive proof of independence. Audit-of-audit access and resource accounting must similarly terminate at bounded existing duties, not create an infinite same-operation chain.

If the configured identity/Policy/evidence/resource dependency graph has no usable independent terminating basis, dependent startup fails with PolicyViolation/DependencyFailure and an attributable safe disposition where possible. No self-authorized skip, inactive Plugin ordinary invocation or promotion of an untrusted replacement is allowed. Required pre-effect root evidence cannot be deferred merely because startup would otherwise be inconvenient.

ADR-009 decides this custody behavior and termination condition. **ADR-006 instantiates the actual executable bootstrap/root dependency graph and lifecycle treatment.** This is a downstream obligation, not a reverse dependency. Similarly, ADR-007 will realize final handoff ordering without redefining what a custody acknowledgment means. If the root is compromised, replacement/recovery needs independently entitled authority outside the compromised basis; absent that, affected work remains blocked.

For Plugin bootstrap, the entitled admission authority identifies the exact candidate release/configuration and required validation evidence, obtains qualifying evidence from its designated entitled producers, and requires matching custody before any protected admission/initialization effect that depends on it. The candidate may supply declarations or other explicitly permitted self-attestations, but cannot turn its own claim into independently authoritative evidence that admits or trusts itself. Evidence checks producer/source entitlement and the required independence; the admission owner decides admissibility. A missing independent basis blocks dependent bootstrap. This is a custody walkthrough, not selection of ADR-006's executable root graph.

## 13. Bounded audit load and failure behavior

Evidence production, verification, retrieval and repair consume resources. Exact per-request payload/record limits, capacity and retention parameters must be declared and governed for a realization; this ADR invents no byte quota or universal rate. Resources retains authoritative reservation/accounting. Evidence cannot grant itself unlimited storage or recursively consume unbounded audit capacity.

Apply bounded admission/backpressure before protected work when mandatory capture capacity/guarantees cannot be supported. Persist the post-effect duty before handoff, and provision the required obligation/capture capacity under the declared failure assumptions. A source cannot claim it will safely record a mandatory obligation after effect while depending on unreserved, unqualified storage that may already be full. Actual allocation/order is ADR-007; inability to establish it blocks dependent effects.

After an effect, capacity/outage failures do not erase it. Existing durable obligations survive; record actual known facts when storage permits and expose gaps/uncertainty. A storage loss beyond the declared failure domain may destroy records: report that limit honestly, not a guarantee that every catastrophic failure can itself be durably logged. No silent sampling/drop of mandatory evidence; only explicitly optional telemetry may be omitted with permitted gap reporting. Retry is bounded by current authority, deadlines, resources and obligation disposition, not an infinite audit loop.

| Condition | Canonical reporting |
|---|---|
| Required evidence/custody/control missing or unacceptable | PolicyViolation/conditions unmet with safe obligation reference |
| Evidence provider/store fails | DependencyFailure with safe specific cause; unknown outcome where applicable |
| Unauthorized issuer/reader | AuthorizationDenied |
| Missing/wrong tenant or unauthorized crossing | TenantViolation as applicable; no existence leakage |
| Malformed/wrong-subject mandatory submission | ValidationError or more specific authority/scope category |
| Conflicting identity/content/expected revision | Conflict |
| Unsupported interpretation/version | VersionMismatch |
| Resource/deadline exhaustion | BudgetExceeded/DeadlineExceeded; prior effects separate |

No universal EvidenceFailure error or new Execution state is added. Fail closed concerns the affected protected action/dependency scope. Unaffected capabilities can continue only if their own mandatory evidence and authority requirements are met.

## 14. Failure timelines and structural examples

### Timeline 1 — Pre-effect custody failure

1. Exact Tool action requires committed recoverable audit E.
2. Source records preparation/submission duty; Evidence cannot commit due to an outage.
3. Dispatcher lacks the required acknowledgment and releases no effect.
4. Parent reports the unmet evidence prerequisite and other already committed claims separately; consumed Approval is not automatically refunded. Repairing evidence later does not automatically authorize delayed dispatch.

### Timeline 2 — Post-effect custody failure

1. Invoker durably records intended effect and post-effect evidence duty before handoff.
2. External effect completes and its source observation is committed.
3. Evidence custody fails; source result remains completed for that named effect, with unresolved custody/postconditions.
4. Repair resubmits the retained observation, never the external action. Parent completion is assessed against all its postconditions, not rewritten effect history.

### Timeline 3 — Lost custody acknowledgment

1. Submission O1 causes Evidence E1 and its retained outcome to commit.
2. Response is lost; source obligation remains unresolved, and pre-effect action stays blocked if it needs the receipt.
3. Source queries/retransmits equivalent O1 with original meaning under current disclosure authority.
4. Evidence returns the prior permitted E1 outcome within coverage. Conflicting new content under O1/E1 fails; no second producing effect or fabricated fresh observation.

### Timeline 4 — Crash in the observation gap

1. Effect intent/evidence duty is durable; external handoff occurs.
2. Process dies before persisting the external response.
3. Restart discovers the duty but cannot assume completed or none.
4. Reconcile provider/source facts; record new observation with truthful event/recording times and limits. Missing detail stays missing; a repair date is not falsely presented as original capture time.

### Timeline 5 — Wrong subject or stale receipt

1. Custody receipt names action A/config R1.
2. Prepared effect changes to B or material config R2, or a required current predicate changes.
3. Old receipt cannot satisfy the changed exact requirement.
4. Obtain correct evidence and current joint applicability, or block. Receipt possession never grants Permission.

### Timeline 6 — Corruption, expiry or restore

1. E1 was acknowledged under a bounded guarantee.
2. Later retrieval detects corruption, permitted expiry or an unverified older restored image.
3. Preserve available custody/reference history; do not claim intact current content or substitute latest.
4. Gate claims/actions requiring unavailable proof, reconcile coverage and disposition under current policy. Independent restore/freshness qualification is required, not trust in the same old image.

### Timeline 7 — Root cycle

1. Candidate Evidence Plugin requires its own not-yet-admitted evidence service to admit itself.
2. Dependency analysis finds no independently designated usable root duty.
3. Startup refuses dependent use; candidate cannot waive its audit or attest itself trusted.
4. An entitled external designation/qualified bounded root can later terminate the graph under ADR-006. Mere logging by the candidate cannot.

### Timeline 8 — Duplicate and correction

1. E1 records source L8 as observed by producer P.
2. Equivalent submission repeats: return original custody; conflicting content under E1: Conflict.
3. New evidence shows the observation was incomplete: commit linked correction E2 with new provenance.
4. Keep original/correction meanings distinguishable subject to retention, and do not silently change Plugin L8 from Evidence.

### Timeline 9 — Retention conflict and confidential review

1. Deletion and retention requirements conflict without an accepted precedence basis.
2. Evidence cannot independently choose deletion or permanent retention; create an entitled time-bounded resolution/review obligation and preserve only permitted state.
3. Reviewer obtains only currently authorized evidence/lineage; unrelated caller receives safe category/correlation, not private tenant reasons.
4. Authorized disposition preserves permitted minimal reference semantics; old identity never points at replacement content.

### Timeline 10 — Crash after Evidence commit

1. Submission O1 causes Evidence E1, its exact custody metadata and retained submission outcome to commit durably under the declared failure model. Evidence-owner authoritative truth is committed custody.
2. Runtime crashes. The source may have recorded the receipt and closed its obligation, or may still know only that O1's result is unknown. Missing source acknowledgment is not proof that custody failed.
3. After restart with intact qualified storage, Evidence recovers E1 and O1's retained outcome; the source independently recovers its fact, obligation and any recorded receipt/disposition.
4. An authorized reconciler joins the exact O1/E1/subject/revision references through status inquiry or equivalent retransmission within declared coverage. It does not create a duplicate record merely because the source lacks the receipt, and never repeats the producing effect.
5. Matching custody meeting the original obligation can close an outstanding source obligation through its guarded update. Already closed obligations are not applied twice. Unavailable/expired outcome coverage remains explicit and requires further governed reconciliation, not an assumption of no commit.

### Timeline 11 — Evidence repair after restart

1. Source fact S8 and its mandatory post-effect evidence obligation exist durably; required custody is still outstanding when the runtime restarts.
2. A bounded repair worker establishes its current Principal/Tenant/permission context and discovers the exact obligation through authorized owner enumeration, without depending on an old in-memory queue.
3. It recovers S8's retained revision and interpretation and checks prior submission status where needed. It submits or safely retransmits evidence of S8; it does not replay the business effect or change S8 to the current head.
4. The record retains original source/event timing if known, and separately identifies later repair and custody timing, reason, uncertainty and limitations. Late custody never masquerades as contemporaneous capture or satisfies a pre-effect barrier retrospectively.
5. If original data cannot be recovered, record the missing scope and keep the obligation unresolved or under explicit failed/requires-intervention disposition. Do not fabricate, substitute latest or claim full proof. Only a matching sufficient custody outcome or entitled disposition can update the obligation; an irrecoverable gap remains visible in dependent claims.

### Timeline 12 — Unauthorized Evidence query

1. Caller knows Evidence ID E1 or submission O1 but lacks current disclosure authority, or supplies wrong/missing TenantContext, and requests content, status, provenance or a repair view.
2. Evidence applies ADR-005 current scope/access checks before disclosure. Reference possession and an earlier successful submission are not Permission; tenant is never inferred from E1.
3. No protected content, existence, tenant/Principal/resource metadata, hidden counts, Grant/Plugin/SecretRef information or provenance is disclosed through results, indexes or error differences. Return safe AuthorizationDenied/TenantViolation semantics as applicable with permitted receiver correlation.
4. Lost-acknowledgment reconciliation follows the same rule: no prior result is exposed solely because the caller knows O1/E1. Non-disclosure does not erase custody or make repetition of the source effect safe.
5. Authorized internal review may obtain more detail only within its separately established current scope; no diagnostic or repair bypass is implied.

The same flow supports deterministic direct Capability state changes: source owner commits its exact revision and duty, Evidence accepts scoped custody, and protected use enforces the requirement. No AI/Workflow driver is needed. Plugin activation, Grant changes, resource claims and Approval consumption are separate source-owner facts; evidence links them without combining their state machines or creating a global clock.

### 14.1 Security threat review

| Threat | Required response and limit |
|---|---|
| Evidence forgery or unauthorized submission | Verify current submitter and entitled source/producer, subject/revision and evidence class before treating the assertion as qualifying proof; possession of a source ID is insufficient |
| Evidence poisoning by Agent/model/Plugin | Text such as “authorization approved” remains an untrusted assertion unless the required producer identity/authority/source/version and verification conditions are satisfied. Storing it cannot create authorization or authoritative source truth |
| Unauthorized query/cross-tenant metadata leakage | Protect content, ID/status/search/count/repair queries and graph traversal; scope indexes and errors as §11 and timeline 12 require |
| Evidence deletion/tampering | Protected revisioned disposition, qualified integrity verification, immutable meaning and linked corrections; a storage row alone is not trusted provenance |
| Storage rollback | Gate affected scope, recover exact coverage and reconcile against trustworthy evidence; a self-consistent old image cannot prove current custody or restore authority |
| Producer compromise | Reassess source entitlement and affected evidence/claims under entitled authority, preserve known limitations and counterevidence; past assertions do not become trustworthy merely because custody succeeded |
| Custodian compromise | Restrict affected use and seek independent entitled recovery/qualification; the compromised custodian cannot prove its own repair by self-attestation |
| Raw credential/Secret leakage | Minimize safe references and bound disclosure/custody; exclude ordinary raw credentials, Secret bytes and private reasoning under §11 |
| Bootstrap recursion/self-admission | Independently designated finite root duties; no candidate-produced statement can establish the candidate's own admission authority |
| Retention conflict | No invented Q-05 priority or indefinite retention; accountable timely disposition under §11 |
| Repair tampering/escalation | Exact obligation and source revision, bounded current Principal/Tenant authority, conditional owner updates, no source-state rewrite or original-effect replay |
| Post-effect observation/evidence gap | Durable pre-handoff obligation, honest unknown/missing facts, exact-source repair or explicit irrecoverable limitation |

These controls are architectural requirements, not claims that ADR-009 alone eliminates compromise, leakage or data loss. Integrity/isolation, enforcement and failure guarantees still need qualified implementation evidence.

## 15. Package mapping, migration and Type-1 cost

`legoai.evidence` holds public custody/provenance contracts and `legoai.reference.evidence` their initial realization. `legoai.observability` and `legoai.reference.observation` retain trace/telemetry. Governance supplies authority/Policy; source implementations retain their own facts/obligations; runtime composition wires contracts without becoming a custodian. Kernel retains shared anchors only. No new package, Python class, universal logging operation or peer implementation dependency is introduced.

Replace storage/custodian through a governed migration preserving scoped identities, original source/interpretation references, producer/custody histories, correction/disposition links, access/retention requirements, retained submission outcomes/coverage and unresolved obligations. Preserve external references or explicit meaning-preserving mappings; never renumber E1 into unrelated evidence. Verify actual content and interpretation, not merely record counts or digests.

Initially prefer an authorized quiescent/guarded cutover over inventing distributed live migration. Each owner transfers its own state, records incremental progress and gates unsupported mixed versions. New custodian must actually accept the necessary guarantees before obligations switch; copied files alone are not new custody acknowledgment. Source owners reconcile pending submissions across the change. Old historical acknowledgments remain attributed to the old realization and failure scope, not relabeled as having always enjoyed the new guarantees.

Moving to separate stores/remote custody adds network/partition and independent durability proof but preserves handoffs. Stronger tamper-evidence or archival storage requires explicit new guarantee qualification and retention/access review. Once new work has committed, reversal is another reconciled migration, not restoring an older image and losing submissions. Read-only historical compatibility can remain even when new writes require a newer representation.

This is Type 1 because evidence IDs support durable claims, source histories and obligations span owners, deletion is irreversible, confidentiality cannot be undone, and missing observations cannot always be recreated. Interface neutrality reduces code coupling, not the cost of migrating proof or establishing trust in past data.

Retention can span multiple releases, so migration must preserve permitted historical interpretation and security/privacy constraints across those releases, not merely the current deployment's data. The Evidence store is never the runtime-wide source of truth: no evidence replay reconstructs or owns all domain state, no universal audit-event requirement is imposed on every transition, and no global append sequence, central audit journal or total order of runtime actions is required. Exact owner revisions and causal links remain sufficient for the declared scope; domain recovery stays with ADR-004's source owners.

Benefits: exact attribution, truthful pre/post-effect failure handling, finite evidence roots, inspectable recovery and provider-neutral custody. Costs: additional durable records/handoffs, explicit repair work, capacity planning, retention/access administration and Type-1 migration effort. No unsupported numerical performance or development-time scores are claimed.

Accepted risks and mitigations: shared-store correlated loss is disclosed and stronger guarantees require a different qualified realization; hostile-host tamper resistance is not claimed and unsupported threat scope is refused; post-effect observation loss remains possible outside a proven capture boundary and must yield uncertainty/gaps; custody outages reduce dependent availability rather than fail open; unresolved retention disputes require entitled timely disposition; audit overload uses admission/backpressure and bounded repair rather than dropping mandatory data.

Reconsider the realization on measured evidence volume/latency, slow recovery, mandatory independent custody, external retention/residency duties, expanded hostile infrastructure, remote/multi-region operation or stronger capture guarantees. Options may include separately durable custodians or append-oriented internals, but none changes source ownership, exact identity, confidentiality, pre-effect barriers or failure truthfulness. Measurements and actual integrity/crash tests remain future proof work.

Specific triggers include independent write-once/read-many (WORM) custody, very high evidence volume, a multi-service runtime, cross-region durability, hostile infrastructure, external audit authority, regulatory retention or cryptographic transparency requirements. Each requires new qualification and possibly a changed realization; none is selected or built by this completion pass.
